THE VISIONARY

Elon Musk is a technology entrepreneur and investor, widely regarded as the most consequential innovator of the 21st century. He is the CEO and lead designer of SpaceX, CEO and product architect of Tesla, Inc., and owner of X (formerly Twitter).

Born in South Africa, his early entrepreneurial journey included founding Zip2 and X.com, which eventually became PayPal. After selling PayPal, he funneled his wealth into his true passions: space exploration and sustainable energy.

Driven by a mission to make humanity multi-planetary and transition the world to sustainable energy, Elon has disrupted multiple industries. From electric vehicles to reusable rockets, his companies are pushing the boundaries of what is physically possible.

Beyond transportation, he is pioneering brain-computer interfaces with Neuralink and revolutionizing underground tunneling with The Boring Company. He constantly warns about the existential risks of Artificial Intelligence while simultaneously building some of the most advanced AI systems in the world.

His relentless drive, controversial public persona, and unwavering commitment to saving humanity from extinction have made him one of the most influential and talked-about figures of our time.
